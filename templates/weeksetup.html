Canvas is our most important resource -- we use it to upload assignments, lectures, and slides from lessons

Sign up for our course here:

Jupyter Lab and Google Colab is a super easy way to get going with code right in your browser

We take notes in both Jupyter Lab and Colab during class and encourage you to follow along.

Unfortunately, school computers are unable to access Colab but Jupyter Lab offers most of the same features as Colab.

Try Colab (make sure to press File -> New Notebook): https://colab.research.google.com/

Try Jupyter Lab (X out of the first notebook that pops up and press Pyodide): https://jupyterlite.github.io/demo/lab/index.html
