What We Teach

  • Neil Agarwal

    Neil will be an incoming high schooler this coming school year. He’s always loved computer science and wants to share that love with others.

    He has experience in many popular programming languages such as Java, Python, C++ Swift, Javascript, HTML, CSS and systems like Rest APIs, Flask, and SQL.

  • Adit Agarwal

    Adit is currently a senior in college at the University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign studying Computer Science.

    He’s a Blue Valley alumnus and has had similar experience teaching programming classes in the past and wants to continue teaching others and introducing them to the world of computer science.

  • Why We Founded Byte By Byte

    A few semesters ago when Adit enrolled in college, he found that many of the students in the CS program had gone to out-of-state, private high schools which had focused almost exclusively on computer science and STEM education.

    As a result, many of these students had years of prior programming experience at these schools, giving them a good advantage during their first few semesters.

    We wanted this level of education to be more accessible to the general public, leading to the foundation of Byte By Byte.

  • What We Teach

    Our primary mission at Byte By Byte is to train tomorrow's programmers, today.

    To achieve this goal, we begin by teaching you the necessary fundamentals of computer science with techniques ranging from algorithms to data structures through the Java programming language. Along with these fundamentals, we’ll also cover the nuances of Java, and more generally, Object Oriented Programming (OOPs) concepts.

    Every week this summer, we’ll hold meetings which involve lectures, hands-on programming practice, group challenges, and tailored assignments.

    We hope that these lessons will be a good stepping stone for you into the programing world and we will support you as much as possible as we embark on this journey together.
